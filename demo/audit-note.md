# Third party audit of the Q3 submission

This page is part of the PACT repository. It plays an independent audit that contradicts the
delivered report, so the demonstration has a real disagreement to adjudicate rather than a single
account of events.

## Findings

Companies present in the submission at the deadline: **31**, not the 50 the agreement requires.

Of the 31, **fourteen** carry fewer than three citations.

Two citations in the submission name documents that the publishers do not carry, at the addresses
given and at the publishers' own indexes. On this evidence those two citations are fabricated.

The submission was received on 2026-09-24T09:00:00Z, before the deadline.

Audited on 2026-09-25.
