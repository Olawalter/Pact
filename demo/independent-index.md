# Open index: verification summary for the Q3 submission

This page is part of the PACT repository. It plays an independent index that checked the same
submission, so the demonstration has a second publisher rather than one party's word.

## What was checked

A sample of 20 of the 52 companies in the Q3 submission was re-checked against the publishers named
in their citations.

## Findings

Companies counted in the submission: **52**.

Required fields were present for every company in the sample.

Every company in the sample carried **three or more** citations that resolve to the publisher named.

No citation in the sample named a document that the publisher does not carry.

Checked on 2026-09-24.
